Justice for Jackson Mueller | Witness Outreach Campaign
This repository contains all public campaign materials created to help bring full justice for Jackson Daniel Mueller, a 15-year-old honors student and athlete from Brick, New Jersey, who was tragically killed on May 19, 2026.
While riding his bicycle through a marked crosswalk near Lanes Mill Road and Rhode Island Avenue around 6:50 AM on his way to Brick Memorial High School, Jackson was struck by a vehicle. A juvenile driver was arrested and charged with knowingly leaving the scene of a fatal motor vehicle crash. However, additional witness testimony and information remain critical to strengthening the prosecution’s case and ensuring complete accountability.
Repository Purpose
This open resource hub provides high-quality, ready-to-use materials for the community, activists, and supporters who want to help amplify the call for witnesses. All assets are designed to be respectful, factual, and focused solely on directing tips to official law enforcement channels.
Included Assets:

High-visibility street posters (11x17" and 18x24" print-ready)
Digital social media graphics and Facebook-optimized images
QR code linking directly to the Brick Township Police anonymous tip form
Facebook page bio, post templates, and Stories content
Witness appeal flyers in multiple formats
Branding guidelines and color schemes (navy, gold, white)

How to Help

Download and print posters
Share digital assets on social media
Post flyers in your local Brick community (with proper permissions)
Contribute new designs or translations (pull requests welcome)

Important Note: This project is not affiliated with law enforcement. All tips should be submitted directly to:

Brick Township Police Tip Line: 732-262-1100
Ocean County Prosecutor’s Office: 732-929-2195

Goal: Honor Jackson’s memory, support his family, and help secure justice by encouraging anyone with information — no matter how small — to come forward.
Jackson’s life mattered. One voice can make the difference.
